First-person rhythm × aim training

Stop moving.
Then shoot on the beat.

CounterBeat turns the single most important movement skill in tactical shooters into a first-person rhythm game. Strafe to the lit window, tap the opposite key to kill your momentum, put your crosshair on the bot and click the instant the beat lands. Move too fast and your shot sprays. Click off the beat and you lose your combo.

The mechanic

Three things have to be true at once

Every note is judged on position, velocity and timing. Miss any one of them and the shot does not count.

1

Position

A bot steps out behind one window in the wall. You have to be standing in front of that window to have a line of sight — from anywhere else the shot hits the wall.

2

Velocity

Your shot spreads with speed, exactly like running inaccuracy in a tactical shooter. A counter-strafe is the only way to reach zero velocity quickly enough.

3

Timing & aim

The shot must land on the bot inside the timing window around the beat. Head hits pay 1.25×; the windows tighten with every difficulty.

Daily challenge

One chart a day, same for everyone

Every day at midnight UTC a new chart is drawn from the library. It has its own leaderboard, so a fresh account can top the table by tonight.

Your songs

Bring your own music

Drop in an MP3 and CounterBeat finds the beat and writes a chart at every difficulty. Songs stay in your browser; scores on them count as personal bests.
