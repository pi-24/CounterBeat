How to counter-strafe

In tactical shooters your bullets only go where you are aiming when you are standing still. Releasing the movement key is far too slow — you slide for a third of a second. Pressing the opposite key cancels your momentum almost instantly. That is a counter-strafe, and it is the whole game here.

The four-step loop

  1. Read the window. Two beats before the note lands, one window in the wall lights up, a bot steps out behind it and a ring starts closing on its chest.
  2. Strafe. Hold A or D to run in front of that window. The velocity bar at the bottom of the screen fills up.
  3. Counter-strafe. Just before you arrive, tap the opposite key and let go. The velocity bar snaps into the green zone and your spread collapses.
  4. Aim and fire on the beat. Put the crosshair on the bot — the head pays extra — and left-click (or press Space) as the ring closes.

What the game is measuring

  • Line of sight through the lit window?Required
  • Crosshair on the bot?Required
  • Speed under 20 u/sPin-point
  • Speed under 65 u/sTight
  • Speed under 140 u/sLoose
  • Anything fasterSpray — miss

Your shot spreads with speed — standing still it is a laser, at a sprint it can land seven degrees wide. Stopping with a counter-strafe rather than just releasing the key earns a 1.5× bonus on that note, and a head hit pays 1.25× on top.

Timing the tap

At top speed a counter-strafe takes roughly a tenth of a second. Hold the opposite key for too long and you start moving the other way; let go too early and you are still sliding.

Too short

Still sliding

You released before your speed reached zero. The shot sprays wide and you keep drifting out from in front of the window.

Just right

Dead stop

Speed hits zero and stays there. The velocity bar turns green and the crosshair is a laser. Fire now.

Too long

Over-corrected

You held the key past zero and started accelerating the other way — often straight behind the wall.

Controls

  • Pause (releases the mouse)EscP
  • Restart levelR

Sensitivity is entered the Counter-Strike way and shown with its Valorant equivalent and cm/360, and the crosshair is yours to shape — both live under Settings on the level-select screen.

Scoring

  • Perfect — 100 points. Pin-point speed and inside the timing window.
  • Good — 60 points.
  • Loose — 30 points.
  • Counter-strafe bonus — 1.5× on any note you stopped for with a counter-strafe.
  • Headshot — 1.25× when the shot lands on the bot's head.
  • Combo — builds to a 2× multiplier at 50 notes, and resets on a miss.

Missing drains your focus bar. Empty it and the run ends early.
