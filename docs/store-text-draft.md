## Short description

> Strafe, stop dead, shoot on the beat. CounterBeat is a first-person rhythm shooter built on the counter-strafe: stop in time with the music, land the shot on the beat, and chase the clean click of a perfect stop.

## Long description

**Stop. Time. Aim.**

CounterBeat takes the most satisfying move in tactical shooters, the counter-strafe, and turns it into a rhythm game. Strafe
with A and D, tap the opposite key to kill your speed, and fire on the beat at a target that steps into a lit window. Stop too
late and the shot sprays. Fire off the beat and it counts for less. Do all three right and you get the click.

**Learn it by playing it**
- A short guided tutorial teaches Stop, Time and Aim, and can be skipped or replayed at any time.
- A speed arc around the crosshair shows your exact speed against the Perfect, Good and Loose stop thresholds.
- Every shot tells you why it was graded the way it was: too fast, too early, too late, off target.
- The results screen shows a timing-error histogram and your average early or late offset, so you can see what to fix.

**Play your way**
- **Campaign:** six tracks across Easy, Normal and Hard. Earn up to three stars per chart (pass, rank A or better, a clean-stops challenge) to unlock the next track and cosmetics. Campaign never fails you out.
- **Quick Play:** any unlocked track and difficulty. Hard and Expert end the run if your Focus runs out.
- **Daily:** one generated chart for everyone, every day.
- **Ranked:** Hard and Expert runs with server-verified leaderboards.
- **Practice:** 0.75x speed, loop a section, live timing readout. Earns nothing, so there is no pressure.
- **Custom (Beta):** bring your own constant-tempo song. Unranked.

**Made to be read**
- The approach ring on each target is the timing cue, and effects never cover it.
- A colour-blind-safe judgement palette with a distinct icon per grade, reduced-flash and reduced-effects options, an on-screen beat cue, separate music, effects and hit-sound volumes, full rebinding, and calibration for your audio setup.
- Built for mouse and keyboard. [Controller: the build has gamepad menus, play and rebinding (task T20), but the GDD v2 scope lists gamepad as out of scope and no live pad run on the Steam build is recorded. Add a controller line only after the creator confirms it.]
