CounterBeat / target bot redesign

Five bots to counter-strafe and shoot

Each concept is drawn as a real 96×128 sprite cell with the game's 10-frame layout: idle 0–3, hit 4–5, death 6–9. Pick a skin, click any frame to inspect it at 3×, and toggle the hitbox to see what counts as a body or head shot.

Hitbox note. The zones are taken from src/game/rules/aim.js (HITBOX) with botHeight 2.0, so the sprite is 1.5 × 2.0 world units (64 px per unit). bodyHalfWidth 0.46 and headRadius 0.22 are absolute world units, not fractions of the cell width. In cell pixels: body box x 18.6–77.4, y 30.7–125.4 (59 × 95 px); head disc centre (48, 20.5), radius 14.1 px. Every concept below is built to that real box, not the wider one in the brief, so a shot at painted pixels is a hit and a shot at empty space is a miss. Animation timings in the preview match TargetField.js: idle 170 ms per frame, hit 55 ms, death 70 ms after a kill.
